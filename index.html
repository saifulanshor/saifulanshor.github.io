Faceless Upload

Internal publishing tool for two Indonesian educational short-video channels run by the same creator: Pantes Aja (everyday science and regional facts) and Kata Ordal (history and the stories behind places and gadgets).

What it does

  • Uploads finished short videos, with their titles and descriptions, to the creator's own YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok accounts.
  • Is used only by the channel owner. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.

Channels

  • Pantes Aja: YouTube "Pantes Aja!", TikTok @pantesaja.id, Instagram @pantesaja_id
  • Kata Ordal: YouTube "Kata Ordal", TikTok @kataordal, Instagram @kataordal, Facebook kataordal.id

Alat internal (Bahasa Indonesia)

Faceless Upload adalah alat internal untuk mengunggah video pendek buatan sendiri ke akun milik sendiri (Pantes Aja dan Kata Ordal). Alat ini tidak dipakai orang lain dan tidak mengakses akun orang lain.
