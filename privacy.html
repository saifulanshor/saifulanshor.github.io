Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Faceless Upload ("the app") is a private, internal tool that the owner of the Pantes Aja and Kata Ordal channels uses to publish their own videos. It is not offered to the public. This policy explains what data the app touches and why.

1. Who uses the app

Only the channel owner, signing in with their own accounts. The app has no other users and offers no sign-up.

2. Data the app accesses

  • Google / YouTube: with the owner's consent, the app uses the YouTube Data API (scope youtube.upload, and youtube.readonly to confirm which channel is connected) to upload videos with their title, description and settings to the owner's own channel. It reads the channel name only to confirm the right channel is connected.
  • Meta (Instagram, Facebook): it publishes Reels to the owner's own Instagram professional accounts and Facebook Pages.
  • TikTok: it posts videos to the owner's own TikTok accounts through the Content Posting API.

The app does not read, collect or store viewers' data, comments, subscriber lists, messages, or any other person's account data.

3. How data is stored

  • Access and refresh tokens are kept only in the owner's private configuration (environment variables / secret storage). They are never published, sold or shared.
  • Video files are stored temporarily in the owner's private cloud storage so the platforms can fetch them, and are deleted automatically after 7 days.

4. Sharing

Data is not shared with third parties, except that videos and their text are sent to the platform they are being published on (YouTube, Instagram, Facebook or TikTok). Use of data received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

5. Third-party terms

By using YouTube features through this app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google handles data under the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time:

  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Meta: Facebook Settings → Business Integrations
  • TikTok: Settings → Security → Manage app permissions

Revoking access stops the app immediately. The owner then deletes the stored tokens.

7. Contact

Questions: send a direct message to @pantesaja_id or @kataordal on Instagram.
